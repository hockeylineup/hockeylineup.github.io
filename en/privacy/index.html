Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

EspaceLigue (“we”) runs the EspaceLigue app and the espaceligue.ca website. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what your rights are.

The French version of this policy prevails.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information

For any question about your personal information, write to the person in charge of protecting it: info@espaceligue.ca

What we collect

  • Your account: first name, last name, email, and your Apple ID if you sign in with Apple.
  • Your player profile: position, skill level, preferred number, region, and a photo if you add one.
  • Your leagues' activity: attendance, absences, lineups, results and stats.
  • Payments: amounts and their status. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Your device: a notification token, to send you your leagues' notices.
  • The waiting list: your email, and your league's name if you give it.

Why

To make the app work: organize games, find subs, process payments and send you the notifications you chose. We don't sell your information and don't do targeted advertising.

Who sees it

  • Your leagues' managers and members see what's needed to play: your name, your position, your attendance.
  • If you're in the sub pool, managers in your region see your name, position and skill level.

Where

Your information is hosted in Canada.

How long

As long as your account exists. You can delete it at any time in the app (My account ▸ Delete my account): your information is then erased, except what the law requires us to keep (payment records, for example).

Your rights

You can ask to access your information, have it corrected or have it deleted, by writing to info@espaceligue.ca. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
