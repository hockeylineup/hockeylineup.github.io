FAQ

What is EspaceLigue?

An app to run a hockey league: schedule, lineups, absences, subs and payments, in one place for the manager and the players.

Which devices?

iPhone and Android. Players get their game notifications right on their phone.

How do I find a sub?

When a player can't make it, the league's sub pool is notified. The first one available answers, and the manager confirms them.

How do players pay?

By card or Apple Pay, in the app. The manager sees who has paid, and can also record a payment received another way.

Can a player be in several leagues?

Yes. One account, one home screen for all their leagues.
