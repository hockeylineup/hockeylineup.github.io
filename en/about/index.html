Who we are

We're two hockey guys from Quebec. Like lots of people, we play in beer leagues. And like lots of managers, we've seen what it takes to hold them together: the schedule in an Excel file, absences by text, subs found at the last minute, money collected one player at a time.

We built EspaceLigue to make running a league simple again. Everything is in one place: the schedule, lineups, attendance, subs and payments, for the manager and the players alike.

The app is made here, in French and English, for rec hockey here.
